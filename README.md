**Development of a cohort state-transition model to estimate district-level yellow fever vaccination coverage in Ghana**


Global vaccine shortages often result in limited access and unequal immunization coverage, hindering outbreak response to vaccine-preventable diseases such as yellow fever (YF). The Eliminate Yellow Fever Epidemics (EYE) strategy has targeted at least 50% immunization coverage for YF in high-risk African countries, such as Ghana, with a recommended threshold of 80% coverage to prevent epidemics. However, sub-national immunization coverage estimates have been challenging in the setting of changing demographics and varying immunization data. Therefore, we developed a district-level, age-structured cohort-state transition model to estimate YF immunization coverage from 2016-2025 across all 261 districts in Ghana. For each district, we used routine annual infant immunization rates, supplementary vaccination campaigns, and age-structured population estimates to predict the cumulative vaccination coverage.
